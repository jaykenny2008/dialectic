Your reading life, beautifully organized

Four simple tools that turn the hours you spend with books into ideas you can actually keep.

Reading timer

Focus sessions with optional ambient sound or white noise. Every minute counts toward your daily goal.

Voice & written notes

Talk through what you just read, or type it out. Transcripts are saved next to the book they belong to.

Thoughtful feedback

Get an analysis of the clarity, logic and comprehension in your reflections, then continue the thread.

Calendar & insights

A month-at-a-glance calendar, a seven-day trend, and a weekly synthesis of everything you've noted.
